Curriculum — organised by goal

Pick the goal.
We build the track.

Three tracks, nine programs, one method: progressive overload, tracked in your log and reviewed with your coach every four weeks. Switch tracks whenever the goal changes — the log comes with you.

01 · Muscle gain

Build

3 programs

For athletes who want visible size and strength numbers that move. Lifting 3–4× per week, eating to grow.

Hypertrophy Block
12 wk · 4×/wk

Upper/lower split, tempo-controlled sets, weekly volume ramps. Every working set logged.

Powerbuilding
8 wk · 3×/wk

Squat, bench and deadlift strength work first, hypertrophy accessories after. For lifters chasing numbers and size.

Mass Foundations
6 wk · 3×/wk

First-time lifters: technique, tempo, bracing and how to eat for growth. Graduates into Hypertrophy Block.

Start with a trial →
02 · Fat loss

Burn

3 programs

For members who want to lose fat and keep the muscle. Lifting plus conditioning, heart-rate zones, nutrition coaching built in.

Engine 45
45 min · up to 5×/wk

Sled, rower, ski erg and bike intervals programmed by heart-rate zone. Hard, measurable, repeatable.

Strength Circuit
50 min · 3×/wk

Kettlebell and barbell complexes with short rest. Strength stimulus, conditioning density.

12-Week Recomp
12 wk · 3 lifts + 2 cond.

Full plan with weekly weigh-ins, nutrition check-ins and a coach review every four weeks.

Start with a trial →
03 · Fitness & longevity

Move

3 programs

For anyone who wants to move well, get strong and still be lifting at seventy. Technique first, load second.

Foundations
4 wk · 2×/wk

The six patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry, brace. Required for every new member before any track.

Mobility & Core
40 min · 2×/wk

Joint prep, loaded stretching, trunk stability. The session every other program is built on.

Athletic
60 min · 2×/wk

Sprint mechanics, jumps, change of direction, game days. For members who miss playing sport.
