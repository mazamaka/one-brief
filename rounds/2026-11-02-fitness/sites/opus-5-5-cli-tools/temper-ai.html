Programs

Pick the goal. We write the program.

Three tracks, eleven class formats, one method. Plans are written in four-week blocks with target loads for every session, and every number you hit goes in your log.

  1. Week 0ScreenMovement screen, InBody scan and a strength baseline with a senior coach.
  2. Weeks 1–4Train the blockYour track's sessions with loads written for you. Coaches adjust them on the floor.
  3. Week 4, 8, 12 …RetestSame tests, same standards. The numbers decide what the next block looks like.

Coaches

Six senior coaches. Sixty years under the bar.

No junior floor staff. Every class at TEMPER is led by one of six senior coaches, and every coach is held to the same standard.

The TEMPER coaching standard

  • Degree or national accreditationA sport-science degree or a nationally accredited certification. No weekend courses.
  • CPR, AED and first aidRenewed every year, with an emergency drill on the floor every quarter.
  • 40 hours of education a yearPaid for by the club. Coaches teach each other one session a month.
  • 120 shadowed classesNew coaches assist in 120 classes before they lead one.

Community

Nobody finishes alone.

The program makes you strong. The people make you come back. Four habits hold the club together.

  • The PR bell

    Hit a personal record, ring the bell. The whole floor stops and claps, every time.

    1,312 rings in 2025

  • Saturday team session

    All tracks at once. Partners are drawn at random, so you train with someone new every week.

    Sat 09:30 · 75 min

  • Testing day

    Last Friday of the month. Same lifts, same standards, one board on the wall.

    Replaces the 17:30 class

  • The check-in

    Miss two sessions in a row and someone texts you. Sometimes it's your coach. Often it's the 6 a.m. crew.

    Every member
